# Research source register — Ireland first

Research review date: 23 November 2026. This is a link register and concise research note, not a substitute for checking the live source.

## Primary Irish authorities

1. Data Protection Commission, **CCTV in Restrooms**  
   https://dataprotection.ie/en/cctv-restrooms  
   Key point: organisations should avoid CCTV where a reasonably high expectation of privacy exists, for example over cubicles. The threshold for CCTV in restrooms more generally remains very high.

2. Data Protection Commission, **Guidance on the Use of CCTV — For Data Controllers**  
   https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/dpc-guidance/guidance-use-cctv-data-controllers  
   Key topics: defined purpose, lawful basis, necessity, proportionality, transparency, security, retention, data processors and accountability. The DPC guidance notes that if supervision or security staff that do not process personal data have proven ineffective, that may indicate CCTV is a proportionate response in an appropriate case; it is not an automatic justification for restroom surveillance.

3. Data Protection Commission, **Data Protection Impact Assessments**  
   https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/organisations/know-your-obligations/data-protection-impact-assessments  
   Key point: DPIA is conducted before processing where required; controller remains accountable; the DPIA may need updating. DPC page includes official sample template.

4. Data Protection Commission, **Prior Consultation — Article 36 GDPR and section 84(3) Data Protection Act 2018**  
   https://dataprotection.ie/en/organisations/know-your-obligations/data-protection-impact-assessments/prior-consultation  
   Key point: where residual high risk cannot be mitigated, consult the DPC before processing. DPC asks for the DPIA and ancillary documents, controller/processor responsibilities, purposes and means, safeguards, DPO contact (if applicable), and further information requested. DPC provides written advice and may exercise other powers. This is not a general permit or blanket authorisation.

5. Data Protection Act 2018 (Irish Statute Book)  
   https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2018/act/7/enacted/en/html

6. GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679  
   https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

7. European Data Protection Board, Guidelines 3/2019 on processing personal data through video devices  
   https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-32019-processing-personal-data-through-video-devices_en
