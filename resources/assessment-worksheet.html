Restroom CCTV
Assessment Worksheet

Ireland-first planning record · version 1.0 · 23 November 2026

Important: This worksheet is a planning aid only. It is not an official DPC form, DPIA, legal opinion, licence or permission to record in a restroom. Irish DPC guidance says the threshold for restroom CCTV is very high and advises avoiding areas where people have a high expectation of privacy, such as cubicles. Obtain independent legal advice. Do not proceed merely because this worksheet is completed.
